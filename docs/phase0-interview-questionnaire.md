# Rev.perakit.ai — Phase 0 User Interview Questionnaire (Pricing Validation)
**Goal:** 20 interviews with experienced IT candidates (3–10 yrs exp, actively interviewing or planning a switch) to validate pricing + willingness to pay.
**Format:** 15–20 min, Hinglish. Show the landing page demo section during the call.
**What NOT to do:** price pehle mat batao — pehle unka pain aur current jugad samjho.

---

## Part 1 — Warm-up (2 min)
1. Abhi kya role hai, kitna experience? Last interview kab diya tha?
2. Agle 3–6 mahine mein switch ka plan hai?

## Part 2 — Pain (5 min)
3. Interview mein sabse zyada darr kis cheez ka lagta hai? (behavioral? system design? live coding? English/Hinglish?)
4. Pichle interview mein koi aisa moment jab blank ho gaye the — kya hua tha?
5. Uski taiyaari ke liye abhi kya-kya use karte ho? (mock interviews, YouTube, notes, dost se practice?)

## Part 3 — Solution reaction (5 min, landing page dikhao)
6. [Demo animation dikhao] "Aisa tool hota jo live interview mein tumhare resume ke hisaab se answer suggest kare — pehla reaction kya hai?"
7. Ismein sabse useful feature kaunsa lag raha hai? Kaunsa bekaar lag raha hai?
8. Koi aisi cheez jo missing lag rahi hai?

## Part 4 — Pricing (5 min) — SABSE IMPORTANT
9. Aise tool ke liye mahine ka kitna dena reasonable lagega? (pehle unse number nikalwao, phir tiers dikhao)
10. [₹0 / ₹499 / ₹999 / ₹299 tiers dikhao] Kaunsa plan loge aur kyun?
11. ₹999/month wala Pro — is price par turant kharidoge ya sochoge? Kya cheez tumhe rok rahi hai?
12. Ek interview crack hone par ye tool kitne ka padna chahiye? (per-interview framing — credits model ka signal)
13. UPI se pay karoge? Subscription ya credits — kaunsa comfortable hai?

## Part 5 — Close (2 min)
14. Agar ye kal launch ho jaye to waitlist mein naam doge? [Form bharwa lo]
15. Koi dost jo interviews de raha hai — intro dila sakte ho? (referral loop)
